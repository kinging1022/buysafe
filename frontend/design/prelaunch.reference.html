Why we’re building this

Somebody always has to go first.

And sometimes they lose.
If you’re buying

You send the transfer, they say “received”. Then the photos stop matching, or the number stops going through.

If you’re selling

You clean it, pack it, cross the city to meet, and the buyer is “on the way” for two hours before going quiet.

How it works

Nobody pays first. The money waits in the middle.

  1. Pay into escrow

    Send a bank transfer to an account number made just for your deal. The seller sees the money is there. Nobody can touch it.

  2. See it, live

    The seller records a live video of the item in the app. Camera only, no gallery. You rate it, or ask to see more.

  3. Meet and check

    Agree on a time and a public place in the app chat. Switch it on, look it over.

  4. Say yes

    Confirm with the item in your hands and the seller is paid straight away.

If it goes wrong

We planned for the bad days first.

The seller doesn’t send a video in time.

The deal is cancelled and your money goes back to the account you paid from, in full.

The video doesn’t look right.

Ask for another one and say exactly what to show. After three, you can cancel for a full refund.

The item isn’t what you saw.

Don’t confirm. Report it in the app. Your money stays held while a real person reviews the deal.
